# Transparency

> **Don't trust, verify.**

Monocle Network operates on a simple principle: **peer privacy is non-negotiable, while network operations are transparent.**

Peers trade privately, while Monocle's own operations remain publicly verifiable. Monocle does not ask peers to trust it — it gives them the tools to verify.

Transparency applies to **network operations only**. Peer wallets, trades, and activity are private by design and are never published.

## Areas of Transparency

Each area below has a dedicated page describing exactly what is published, where, and how to verify it.

Transparency claims are only meaningful if peers can check them independently.

### Appeals Process
Appeal outcomes and reasoning, published on GitHub without identifying information.

Find out more about transparency regarding the [Appeals Process](https://monocle.wiki/Transparency/Appeals_Process).

---

### Canary
A regularly published statement affirming that Monocle Network has not been subject to any legal process, secret orders, or coercion that would compromise the privacy of its peers.

Find out more about transparency regarding the [canary](https://monocle.wiki/Transparency/Canary).

---

### Code Changes
Monocle Network is a fork of Haveno. The changes made to the code are verifiable.

Find out more about transparency regarding [code changes](https://monocle.wiki/Transparency/Code_Changes).

---

### Community Reserve Addresses and View Keys
Addresses and view keys for all three reserves, so anyone can confirm 33% of fees flow to them.
